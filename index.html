How it works

Five steps. Under six months.

No listing. No prep. No dressing it up. You sell it as it is, quietly, on your timeline.

1

One conversation

A relaxed, confidential call. You walk me through the business, and I tell you honestly what I see. No obligation, and it stays private.

2

A discreet visit

We meet in person and I tour the operation, discreetly. As far as your team knows, I'm a prospective client, a supplier, or an advisor. No one learns you're selling until you choose to tell them.

3

A confidential review

You share the financials, operations, and legal details, privately and under NDA, so I can prepare your valuation and offer. Diligence stays in-house. No outside parties poking around.

4

A fair offer

Based on the full picture, I make you a fair, straightforward offer, direct from the buyer. It often includes some seller financing to help it close.

5

We close

Papers and terms arranged, and you're out. Quietly, and in less than 6 months.
